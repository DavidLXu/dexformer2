# What the films demonstrate

These scenes replay selected successful recordings. They do not estimate success rates; failures and the full trial population are outside the scope of this rendering repository.

- **Conveyor:** two recorded target objects per selected trajectory; 24 configurations reused at 60 stations. Staging, visibility and camera choreography are scripted. The source builder includes visual centering and lowered transition adjustments; it is not an unedited benchmark recording.
- **Newton:** analytic apple fall and an artistic tree prologue lead into independently recorded grasps and an edited multi-layer formation reveal. The falling apple is a scripted prologue, not a policy catching experiment.
- **Ring:** 12 selected physical four-object sequences, four arms and five hands. The policy grasps; external IK transfers, scripted opening releases, and low returns connect cycles. RAPID did not pass the selected smooth four-object sequence. A shared variable clock retimes robot and object tracks together.
- **Long table:** 24 separately recorded configurations, rigidly staged along a table. Reveal timing and trajectory playback are choreographed; no shared 24-robot physical scene is claimed.
- **Continuous packing:** 24 verified independent one-object grasp/drop clips are repeated. Recorded robot/object tracks stay paired. Return blends, replenishment, conveyor transport, tray scheduling and repeated cycles are scripted. Seven boxes are spaced five stations apart. This is not a new continuous multi-cycle policy evaluation or a measured real-time throughput result.
- **Apple clips:** separate apple-grasp recordings at half-speed, one per arm–hand combination.

The dynamic speed overlay labels the replayed action's source-time advancement. It excludes camera speed and scripted conveyor speed. In multi-station overview shots it reports a range; independent tracks do not share one global playback multiplier.

Recorded source coordinates, source hashes and selection metadata are retained. Rendering is native 3D geometry; no generative image model invents robot fingers or substitutes object shapes.
